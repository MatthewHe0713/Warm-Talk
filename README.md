# Warm-Talk
## A chat application dedicated to the mental health of the elderly

# Why This Project Matters:
To provide emotional support and companionship for elderly individuals living alone, especially those whose children or family members are not nearby. WarmTalk acts as a virtual friend that chats, listens, comforts, and shares everyday moments through natural conversation.

## Addresses a Real Need: Many elderly people suffer from loneliness, which can lead to mental and physical health issues.
## Technically Feasible: Build a prototype using existing AI tools for speech recognition, text generation, and voice synthesis.
## Meaningful Impact: More than just a chatbot, WarmTalk offers empathy, daily interaction, and emotional connection.

# Main functions and highlights

## 🎭Emotion recognition: Automatically analyze the emotions of user messages (such as happiness, sadness, anger, etc.). And conduct a mental health assessment of the user's language.
## 🛡️Sensitive content detection: When sensitive words related to violence, self-harm, and medical care are detected, an automatic reminder will be given without generating a reply to protect user safety.
## 🤗AI Gentle Response: Based on the results of emotion recognition, OpenAI and LLM models are invoked to generate short, caring, and easy-to-understand responses.

# Social value
## WarmTalk Companion is an AI-powered chat app designed to support the emotional well-being of elderly users. By combining Hugging Face’s emotion detection with OpenAI’s language model, it offers warm, empathetic responses tailored to each user's feelings. The app also ensures safety through built-in detection of sensitive content like self-harm or violence. Beyond individual support, WarmTalk addresses a growing social need—helping reduce loneliness, improve mental health, and foster a sense of connection in aging populations. It demonstrates how technology can be a force for compassion, care, and inclusion in an increasingly digital world.
